### Using AI to Identify the "Single-Problem Focus"

Use **ChatGPT** to scan real user data from community forums and social platforms.

### Copy Structure for Short VSLs (3-Minute Sales Videos)

For a **micro-infoproduct**, your script must be **fast**, **direct**, and **authoritative**. 

Traditional long-form sales copy often uses drawn-out emotional stories. 

Instead, use the [Boring Copy Method](/boring-copy-ai-sales-pages-guide/) to focus on **clinical diagnosis** and **immediate solutions**.
