Your Brand DNA is the most valuable asset you own in the age of AI. Here is how to build it using the CopyMonster framework.

### Mapping the Core
Start with your **History**. Why do you exist? What battlefield was your authority built on? 

### Defining the Value
Move to the **Value Proposition**. What real problem do you solve, and how is it different from everyone else?

### Locking the Voice
Ensure your **Positioning** and **Proof** are clear. This prevents AI from generating "hallucinated" claims about your business.
